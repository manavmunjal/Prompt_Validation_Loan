Personal gifts (Fannie Mae Selling Guide B3-4.3-04)

- Acceptable donors include a relative, a domestic partner, and a fiance or fiancee of the borrower. The donor may
  not be an interested party to the sale (seller, builder, real estate agent).
- A gift letter that the donor signs. It gives the amount, how the donor is related to the borrower, the donor's
  name and contact details, and states that no repayment is due.
- Evidence of the transfer, one of: the donor's check and the borrower's deposit; the donor's withdrawal and the
  borrower's deposit; the donor's check or wire to the closing agent; the settlement statement showing the gift.
- No proof of the relationship is required beyond the gift letter.
