Applicant whose spouse is not on the loan

- Regulation B (12 CFR 1002.5(c)): a lender may not request information about an applicant's spouse unless the
  spouse will use or be liable on the account, the applicant relies on the spouse's income or on alimony or support
  from the spouse, or the applicant lives in a community property state.
- The spouse's income, debts and credit are not part of the application when the applicant qualifies alone.
- State law may require a non-borrowing spouse to sign the security instrument so the lien is valid. That signature
  does not make the spouse a borrower.
